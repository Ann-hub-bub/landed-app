+

No. We sample your past writing, your resume voice, your LinkedIn posts. Every letter goes out in your tone. You review the first batch — and any future tweaks land back in the engine.

Do I have control before applications are sent?

+

Every application is staged for your review first. One click to approve. Or click into the draft, edit, then approve. Nothing leaves without you.

Is it safe to use automation on career sites?

+

We don't scrape or impersonate. We submit through the company's own APIs and email hiring managers directly. Same channel you'd use — just faster and chased.

How do you help me pass ATS filters?

+

The resume is rewritten with the role's actual keywords, salary bands, and titles — pulled from the live posting. Then verified against the company's ATS scoring before submission.

+

Yes — we filter for H1B, OPT, TN, and L1-friendly companies first. Sponsorship status is verified on each posting before it enters your pipeline.
